THE KILN

a gas reduction firing · one night
you fire the kiln at a small community pottery. nine people share the studio, and firing is everybody's work at once — yours and theirs. you can't see in. you can't undo. and at the end you find out what the fire made of it at the same time as everyone else.
1 · load it
nine named shelves, each with its own heat and atmosphere. where you put a piece decides what it becomes. then you brick up the door.
2 · fire it blind
twelve hours on three knobs — gas, air, damper. no window. you steer by a spyhole, a stack of bending cones, and what the kiln sounds like.
3 · open it
it cools for longer than it fired, and you can't open it hot. then you take them out one at a time and find out what you did.
